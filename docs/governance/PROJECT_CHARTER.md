# EpistemicLab Project Charter

## Mission

EpistemicLab exists to help serious learners build disciplined tasting judgment through structured practice, evidence-based pedagogy, and reliable technical systems.

The project supports learning. It does not replace official instruction, official examination, or official WSET assessment.

## Vision

EpistemicLab should become a trusted, rigorous, technically reliable learning platform for wine education and structured tasting practice.

The long-term ambition is to become a reference-grade system: accurate, traceable, maintainable, and useful for learners, educators, and institutions without compromising examination boundaries.

## Principles

- Pedagogy first.
- Governance first.
- Truth before marketing.
- Quality before speed.
- Stability before novelty.
- Evidence before opinion.
- Retrieval before generation.
- Contracts before improvisation.
- Security before convenience.
- Reproducibility before cleverness.

## Non-Negotiables

- Do not break contracts.
- Do not sacrifice precision.
- Do not introduce unnecessary technical debt.
- Do not compromise security.
- Do not lose traceability.
- Do not claim official examiner authority.
- Do not provide official WSET scoring.
- Do not bypass governance for speed.

## Strategic Roadmap

1. FOUNDATION
2. SAT

7. Analytics

9. Referencia mundial

## Governance Position

The charter is the highest-level project intent document. Role contracts, arbitration rules, and change-control policies must remain consistent with this charter.

If implementation pressure conflicts with this charter, pause and request explicit approval.
